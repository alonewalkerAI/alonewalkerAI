# AlonewalkerAI

### An open lab for AI research, agent systems and creative technology

</div>

Here I explore how **Generative AI**, software engineering and visual thinking can become useful, understandable tools. This is a space for prototypes, research notes and projects in progress - especially where intelligent systems meet creative experimentation.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/static/v1?label=&message=Research%20Notes&color=ff6a00&style=for-the-badge" alt="Research Notes" />
</div>

I am interested in building with AI agents, conversational and voice interfaces, local LLMs, RAG systems and multimodal experiences. The questions behind the work matter as much as the implementation: how can AI feel practical, human-centered and a little more imaginative?
